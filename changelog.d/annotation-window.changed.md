- An opt-out annotation may now sit anywhere in the comment block directly above the construct it excuses, and anywhere inside that construct's own source span. The window used to be one line, so a reason that wrapped onto a second line pushed the token out of range and the check still fired. An author who explained WHY was told to reformat. Both `check-toolchain-skips` and `check-case-default` accept the wider window; the block must be unbroken, so a blank line still ends it and an annotation written for something else cannot drift down onto a later construct. The prose checks (`check-graceful-handwave`, `check-historical-comments`, `check-drift-guards`) keep their one-line window: there the flagged line is itself a comment, and one annotation at the top of a paragraph would blanket every later sentence in it. <!-- allow-graceful: names the lint whose banned word this is; it makes no claim about behaviour -->
