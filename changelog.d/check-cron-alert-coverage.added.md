- `check-cron-alert-coverage` (Extras): fails a scheduled workflow whose failures reach nobody — a cron has no PR surface, so its red is visible only to whoever opens the Actions tab. Fails closed: every scheduled workflow must route its failures to a human, by a notification step a failure can reach (`if: failure()`, `needs.x.result != 'success'`), by a failure notifier in the tree listing its `name:` in `on.workflow_run.workflows`, or by a reasoned `# cron-alert: false  # <reason>` marker. A repo that deliberately routes its crons nowhere passes `--allow-unrouted`, which narrows the check to the two lies rather than muting it: a malformed marker, and a notification step behind a success-only gate (`if: success()`, `needs.x.result == 'success'`) that reads as coverage but fires never. `--notifier-pattern REGEX` (repeatable) extends the built-in notifier patterns rather than replacing them.
