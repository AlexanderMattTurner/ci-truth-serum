- `mute-failure-guard` keeps a step running when a launcher unsets the guard's own variables under `set -u`. On bash 3.2, an ERR row names its command `?`, not the guard's own function.
- `mute-failure-guard` prints every ledger row of a failed step, up to 500 rows. The report cut each step to its newest 50 rows, and a long step lost its first failure, which is often the cause.
