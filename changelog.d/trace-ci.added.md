- `trace-ci`: a new console script that follows CircleCI and GitHub Actions steps into the `make` recipes that they run. It runs the content lints of this pack on the commands that CI really executes, and it reports each finding at its source line. It makes each `$(shell …)` call in the makefiles fail, one at a time, and reports a call when `make` then still exits 0 but runs fewer commands or arguments. It also reports a recipe shell that loses a failure in `a; b` or `a | b`, a recipe line that starts with `-`, and a `check` goal that no CI step reaches. It is not a pre-commit lint, and no tier aggregate runs it.
