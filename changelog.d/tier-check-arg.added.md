- `run_tier` (the `check-tier1` / `check-tier2` / `check-extras` aggregates): `--check-arg <check>=<flag>` gives one member check one extra argument, so a parameterised check stays inside its tier instead of being skipped and re-listed as its own hook. Repeat it to pass several. A flag and its value are one token (`--check-arg check_retry_loop=--wrapper=retry_cmd`), because the split is on the first `=` only. The aggregate rejects a flag for a check outside the tier, and rejects a flag for a check `--skip` removes — both would otherwise do nothing while the caller believed the check was configured.
