- **`check-cancellable-required-check`, `check-pending-cancel-concurrency` and `check-required-event-closure`** also treat a job as gating when `.mergify.yml` names its check in a `check-success=<name>` queue or merge condition. A repository that moved to Mergify and deleted its `# required-check: true` markers no longer gets a silent pass. The `merge_group` trigger rule still applies only to marked jobs, since Mergify sends no `merge_group` event.
