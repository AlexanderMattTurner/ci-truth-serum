- `check-full-history-blobs` (Tier 2): the check refuses an `actions/checkout` step with `fetch-depth: 0` and no `filter:` input. Such a step downloads every version of every file, and most jobs read none of them. `--require-filter=<value>` demands one exact filter value. A job that reads file content across history, or pushes or fetches history out of the checkout, opts out with `# full-blobs-ok: <reason>`.
