- `check-flock-fixed-fd` now finds `flock 9` behind a prefix command: `sudo`, `doas`, `command`, `env`, `exec`, `nice`, `nohup` and `time`. Behind `sudo` or `doas`, an `exec 9>FILE` does not exempt the call, because both close fd 9 before `flock` runs. It skips a `NAME=VALUE` operand after `sudo`, and it does not read `sudo -l flock 9`, which lists privileges, as a call. `--wrapper NAME` (repeatable) adds your own wrapper function, and `--no-exec-exemption` also reports the `exec 9>FILE; flock 9` pairing.
