- `check-required-step` (Tier 2): a script that works only after an earlier step declares that step with `# requires-step: <uses value>`. The check follows each `run:` step into the scripts it runs, and refuses a job that has no earlier step with that `uses:` value. The script refuses at run time anyway, but a job that runs only on a schedule or after a merge then fails on the default branch.
