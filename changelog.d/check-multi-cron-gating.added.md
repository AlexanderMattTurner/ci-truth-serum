- `check-multi-cron-gating` (Extras): fails a workflow that declares two or more crons whose jobs cannot tell one cron from another. GitHub starts every schedule-eligible job on EVERY cron fire, so a job gated on the bare `github.event_name == 'schedule'` runs for all of them — the observed cost was a paid eval started by a newly added sibling cron, on a schedule nobody asked for, with the bill as the only signal. In a multi-cron file every schedule-gated job must name its cron (`github.event.schedule == '13 5 * * 1'`), every declared cron must be named by at least one job (at-least-once — several jobs may answer one cron, and one job may name several), and a job with no `if:` must carry a reasoned `# multi-cron-ok: <reason>` marker (a placeholder like "n/a" fails). Single-cron workflows are exempt: the bare event-name gate is unambiguous there.
