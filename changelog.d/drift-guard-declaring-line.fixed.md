- `check-drift-guards`: a phrasing finding now points at the line that declares the guard, and names the hatch that line accepts. A docstring that says "the two tables can't drift apart" used to report the enclosing `def`, several lines above the sentence, and the remedy it printed was `# not-a-drift-guard:` — the structural opt-out, which clears no phrasing hit. The message now offers `# drift-guard-ok: <reason>`, and the check accepts that annotation on a Python test, exactly as it already did in a JS, TS, or shell suite. A structural finding still reports the `def`, because the structure is the whole function. `violations()` returns a `Finding(line, name, route)` in place of a `(line, name)` pair.
